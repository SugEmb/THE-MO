Escape Puzzle Kit Free
======================

The free sampler of Escape Puzzle Kit: its interaction, items and inventory, and doors and locks
systems for first-person games in Unity 6.3+, Built-in and URP, with story beats and saves. Each
system works on its own, wired in the Inspector with UnityEvents, with any first-person
controller. 14 working low poly props, 10 set pieces and a storeroom to escape from.

escape-puzzle-kit-free-1.1.0.unitypackage
    Assets > Import Package > Custom Package. It lands in Assets/EscapePuzzleKitFree/. Start with
    Documentation/README.md, then open Demo/Storeroom.unity and press Play.

Documentation/
    A page per system. Items.md says how to add an item, Props.md how to swap a mesh, and
    CHANGELOG.md what changed from version to version.

The full kit adds switches and power, combination locks and card readers, five close-up
minigames, 100 working props in two looks and two escape levels that play every system and game:
    https://heyheythere.itch.io/escape-puzzle-kit

Sounds: PSX Horror SFX by heyheythere

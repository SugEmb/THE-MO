# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- On Escape Puzzle Kit 1.1.0's runtime and models: every model faces +Z with Y up on an identity
  prefab root, the components' directions and the Storeroom turned to match, and the examine view
  first shows a model's front. Plain-surface UVs straightened on a named 2048x2048 atlas
  (`EscapePuzzleKitFree_BaseColorAtlas`) and its glow map.
- The brass key has a round shank, a collar by the bow and a tip; the padlock a keyhole in a boss.

## 1.0.1

- `Third-Party Notices.txt` names the sounds' pack, in place of their own licence files.
- The scripts compile in a project set to Both without the Input System package installed.

## 1.0.0

- First release: interaction, items and inventory, and doors and locks, with story beats and
  saves, for Unity 6 on Built-in and URP.
- Working low poly props, the props gallery (`Demo/Demo.unity`) and the Storeroom escape
  (`Demo/Storeroom.unity`).
- Sounds on the props, the Hud and the flashlight, from PSX Horror SFX.
